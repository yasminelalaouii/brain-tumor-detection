Types of Brain Tumors

  • Glioma Tumor: Arises from glial cells in the brain or spine. Often malignant and requires aggressive treatment, including surgery, radiation therapy, and chemotherapy.
  • Meningioma Tumor: Forms from the membranes covering the brain and spinal cord. Typically benign and slow-growing, requiring surgery or radiation therapy if recurrence occurs.
  • Pituitary Tumor: Develops in the pituitary gland, affecting hormone production. Treatment options include medication, surgery, and radiation therapy, depending on the type.
